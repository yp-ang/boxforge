mAP50-95 — the headline. >0.5 is strong for a custom dataset,
        0.3–0.5 is usable, <0.2 means a data problem rather than a training problem.

      
mAP50 — looser IoU. High mAP50 with low mAP50-95 means the model
        finds objects but its boxes are sloppy — usually inconsistent annotation tightness.

      
Precision high, recall low — it misses objects. More data, or
        more varied data.

      
Recall high, precision low — false positives. Add background
        images (annotated, zero boxes) showing the scenes where it fires wrongly.
